MIT Media Lab,
and where it meets my work

The MIT Media Lab is a research building where about twenty-four small groups work on things that do not fit in any one department — instruments, materials, satellites, robots, ways of talking to each other. Each group is five to twenty people, usually one professor and their graduate students.

I went through eight of those groups and their neighbours to see what they are currently making. What follows is not a summary of the Media Lab. It is the places where their work and mine turn out to be about the same thing — sometimes closely enough that we have separately built the same object.

Everything here is public information from their own websites, gathered in August 2026. This page is for me, and for anyone who wants to understand what I have been doing and what company it keeps. It is not a ranking and not an argument that these are the same thing — only a record of where the work rhymes.
